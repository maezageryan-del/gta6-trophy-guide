## What it is

**Leonida Trophy Atlas** is the complete, offline guide to all **55 official GTA VI trophies** on PlayStation 5 (1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze; 28 hidden on PSN). Every trophy keeps its official name, description, grade and hidden flag exactly as PlayStation publishes it, and carries a guide written in the author's own words with its sources named beside it (Exophase, PSNProfiles, PowerPyx, GTA Wiki, GTA Forums).

What makes it more than a list:

- **Checklist tracker** — tick trophies off; progress saves in localStorage, with export to file and import back.
- **Big progress header** — X/55 earned, percent, and per-grade counts.
- **Filters** — by kind (story, side strands, activities, collectibles, open world, completion), grade, missable-only, hide-earned, plus full-text search.
- **Spoiler-safe mode** — hides story trophy names and descriptions until you earn them.
- **Platinum roadmap** — seven ordered stages from the first mission to 100% completion and the platinum.
- **Missable alerts** — a visible banner plus a Missables tab; the 8 missable trophies each carry a "when it can be missed" line.
- **Sources tab** — where every fact was checked, and the counts behind the guides.

## How to run

No build step, no network loads — all assets are local.

```sh
node serve.ts        # serves app/ on http://localhost:8080
```

Then open <http://localhost:8080>. Or open `app/index.html` directly in a browser.
